# Privacy Policy: HUSH: Echoes in the Dark

_Last updated: 2026-10-21_

HUSH: Echoes in the Dark ("the game") is an offline single-player game.

**What we collect:** nothing. The game does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and the app does not request internet access.

**What is stored on your device:** your game progress (completed levels, stars, best times, read story logs) and your settings (volume, sensitivity, graphics options). This is kept locally inside the app's private storage and never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings > Reset progress in the game, deletes it.

**Permissions:** the app uses only the vibration permission, to give haptic feedback during play.

**Children:** the game contains horror themes and is not directed to children under 13.

**Changes:** if this policy changes (for example if online features are ever added) the updated policy will be published at the same address and the "last updated" date changed.
