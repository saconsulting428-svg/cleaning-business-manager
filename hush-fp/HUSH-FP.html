How to play

Survive in the dark. Your flashlight shows little. A sonar pulse lights up walls, doors and creatures for a few seconds, but every sound you make is noise, and the creature comes to where the noise was.

Move quietly. Crouching is silent, walking is soft, running is loud. Glass and water are loud. Doors are loud. Generators are very loud.

Find what the exit needs (keycard, key, fuses for the generator, a code, a valve) and escape. Hide in lockers when it comes, keep still, and let it pass. If it saw you go in, it will check.

Use the radio to make noise somewhere else. Vents are too small for it.
