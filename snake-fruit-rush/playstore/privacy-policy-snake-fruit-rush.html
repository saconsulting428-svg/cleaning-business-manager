Privacy Policy – Snake Fruit Rush

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Snake Fruit Rush ("the app") is a casual mobile game developed for entertainment.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, skins, settings and high scores) is saved only on your device. We do not run a server, and the app has no accounts or sign-in.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob (banner ads, interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads). To show and measure ads, AdMob and Google may collect and use certain data, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you are asked for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). This does not remove ads from the app; it only makes them less personalised.

Rewarded ads

Some features (such as a hint, an extra life or bonus coins) are offered in exchange for watching an optional ad. You never have to watch an ad to play.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all data it saved on your device. Data held by Google for advertising can be managed in your Google ad settings.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app changes. The date above shows the latest version.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the app developer through the Google Play Store listing.
