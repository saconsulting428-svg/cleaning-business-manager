## Full description (max 4000)
🏠 BUILD YOUR DREAM HOME — ONE PUZZLE AT A TIME

Welcome to Home Haven 3D, the cozy puzzle game where every win builds your very own house! Clear the plot, lay the foundation, raise the walls, add doors and windows, put on the roof — then decorate every room exactly the way you like. Real 3D building, relaxing puzzles, and a home that is truly yours.

🧩 7 RELAXING PUZZLE TYPES
• Pipe Fix – connect the water tank to every tap
• Nuts & Bolts – sort colorful nuts onto the right bolts
• Tile Pattern – spot the pattern and fill in the missing tiles
• Wire Up – link matching colors without crossing paths
• Brick Fill – fit every block into the gap
• Fuse Box – flip the switches and light every bulb
• Paint Mix – mix the perfect color
Easy to learn, satisfying to solve, and tricky enough to keep your brain happy. No timers, no stress.

🔨 REAL 3D HOUSE BUILDING
• Tap glowing spots and watch your house go up piece by piece, with hammer, dust and sparkle effects
• Build room by room: living room, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom and more
• Add stairs, a second floor, a roof and a garden
• Combo bonuses reward quick, confident building
• Switch to Advanced Build in Dream Home mode for full creative freedom

🛋️ DESIGN STUDIO
• Walk into your rooms and decorate them in 3D
• Drag, rotate and stack furniture
• Choose floor, wall and furniture styles — wood, marble, brick, modern and more
• Take client design briefs, earn stars and compare before & after
• Unlock new sofas, lamps, plants, paintings and so much more

🎁 REWARDS EVERYWHERE
• Win puzzles to earn coins and building materials
• Daily free spin, daily quests and star chests
• Collect achievements and finish chapters of a 7-chapter story
• Unlock new properties and decorate more than one home

📱 MADE FOR YOUR PHONE AND TABLET
• Play puzzles in portrait, build and design in landscape
• Smooth, friendly 3D graphics that run on most devices
• Automatic saving — pick up right where you left off
• Short sessions: perfect for commutes, coffee breaks and bedtime

✨ WHY PLAYERS LOVE HOME HAVEN 3D
• A new tutorial guides you step by step — no confusion
• Hints and undo help you when you are stuck
• Cozy art, gentle music and a relaxing pace
• Free to play — watch optional ads for bonus rewards

Ready to turn an empty plot into your dream home? Download Home Haven 3D now and start building!

Contains ads. Optional rewarded videos give bonus coins, hints and extra moves.
